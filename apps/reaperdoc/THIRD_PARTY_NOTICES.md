[the publication audit](../../docs/ecosystem/publication-audit.md) and

repository for the recorded per-artifact review. The maintainer has confirmed that
the acknowledged sources may be used with attribution, and on 2026-09-17 confirmed
that the Git-only reference corpus may be published with sources attributed. Each
upstream item keeps its own license, unaffected by this MIT scope.
